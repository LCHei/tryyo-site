The Approach

TRYYO helps teams create clarity, alignment, and momentum before bigger investments.

Most teams do not get stuck because they lack ideas.

They get stuck because the real problem is not clear, the goals are not shared, and the next step feels risky.

Through impact framing and design sprints, TRYYO turns complexity into a shared understanding of the challenge, the opportunity, and the decision worth making next.

Services

Focused formats for real progress.

Impact Framing

A workshop to define the real problem, align on outcomes, and choose the opportunity worth pursuing.

  • Align on shared goals
  • Identify critical Pains, Needs, and Desires
  • Select opportunity to drive impact
  • Typically 1-2 days, in person, remote possible

Sprint

Design Sprint is a structured, proven approach to move from challenge and opportunity to a tested concept and reduce the risk of building the wrong thing.

  • Accelerate decision-making and alignment
  • Map critical assumptions to test
  • Validate concept with testing
  • Typically 4-5 days, in person, remote possible
